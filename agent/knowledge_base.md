# Nico's Bank: Emergency Line Policies

Nico's Bank is a fictional digital bank in Chile. This document is the source of truth for the AI emergency line.

## Protection levels

**Full lock.** Used when the customer's phone is lost or stolen, or when there is fraud or a suspected compromised account. The whole Nico's Bank account is locked and ALL cards are blocked: debit, credit and virtual. The phone has the banking app on it, so everything is locked regardless of what else was or wasn't taken.

**Card only.** Used when the customer lost only a physical card (debit or credit) and still has their phone. Only that card is blocked. The account, the other physical card and the virtual card keep working.

## Incident types

- Theft: taken without the customer noticing (e.g. pickpocketing).
- Robbery: taken with force or threats.
- Loss: misplaced; the customer doesn't know where it is.
- Fraud: someone used the account, cards or personal data without permission.

## Required information before locking

RUT (with check digit), incident type, date, approximate time (AM or PM must be clear), and a short description of what happened. Explicit consent is always required before locking.

## Getting back into the account after a full lock

The customer can regain access 1 hour after the lock, once they pass an identity validation in the Nico's Bank app (selfie plus ID card photo). This makes sure the next person using the account is really the customer. Once back in, they can unblock their cards from the app (Cards > select card > Unblock), or keep them blocked and order replacements.

## Replacement cards

- Physical debit or credit card: order from the app (Cards > Replace card). Free of charge for lost, stolen or fraud cases. Delivered in 3 to 5 business days in Santiago, 5 to 8 business days in other regions.
- Virtual card: a new one can be generated instantly from the app once the account is active again.

## Fraud follow-up

Every emergency call creates an incident ticket for the Fraud & Security team. For fraud cases, a specialist contacts the customer within 48 hours to review unrecognized transactions. Disputed charges are reviewed within 10 business days, as required by Chilean regulation.

## Chat and human agents

The emergency line only handles account and card emergencies. For anything else, customers can chat with Nico's Bank in two ways:

- The chat inside the Nico's Bank app (Profile > Help > Chat with us).
- The web chat on the Nico's Bank website, on the same page as the emergency line.

A human agent answers either chat Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 19:00, and Saturdays 10:00 to 14:00 (Chile time). Outside those hours customers can open a ticket through either chat, and the team replies as soon as someone is available.

## What Nico's Bank will never ask for

Passwords, PINs, one-time codes (OTP), full card numbers or CVV. Not by phone, chat, email or SMS.

## Filing a police report

For theft or robbery, customers are encouraged to file a report with Carabineros de Chile (phone 133) or online at the Comisaría Virtual. The report number can be added to the incident ticket later through the web chat. It is not required to lock the account.
